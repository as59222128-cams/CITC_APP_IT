Leave on Proceeding — HPL / EOL

⚠ HPL/EOL deductions applied month-wise in Annexure I. HPL = half daily salary deducted. EOL = full daily salary deducted.
ⓘ HPL/Suspension: Employee receives subsistence allowance — ½(BP+DA) for the leave days (full BP+DA for duty days); HRA is paid in FULL for the first cumulative 180 HPL/Suspension days in the FY, and at HALF (not zero) for any leave days beyond that. EOL: No salary at all for leave days (full pay only for duty days). All other allowances (CCA/AHRA/PHA-PHC/P.Pay/Uniform/Washing/Risk/Ration/Night Shift/Spl.Comp/Other/IR) are duty-period prorated for HPL, Suspension AND EOL alike — the employee can claim these only for the duty-period of that month. Statutory deductions (CPS/APGLI/GIS/EHS/PT/AdvTDS) are auto-computed in Annexure I on the actual (subsistence/duty-period) salary drawn that month — CPS is always based on (BP+DA actually drawn)×10%; if that month's adjusted salary is ≤ the normal monthly deductions (including a ₹0 EOL month), only that reduced CPS is deducted and every other deduction is skipped for that month — otherwise all deductions apply as usual.

Form No. 39 — Relief u/s 157(1), Income-tax Act 2025

[See rule 73, Income-tax Rules 2026] — replaces Form 10E, applicable Tax Year 2026-27 onward

About Form 39
ⓘ Form No. 39 (Rule 73 of the Income-tax Rules, 2026) replaces Form 10E under the Income-tax Act, 2025, and is used to claim relief under Section 157(1) for: additional salary/advance salary, gratuity, retrenchment compensation, or commutation of pension. It applies only from Tax Year 2026-27 onward. For Tax Year 2025-26 (AY 2026-27) and earlier, continue to use Form 10E under the Income-tax Act, 1961 (see the Form 10E tab). Form 39 has two parts — Part A (basic details) and Part B (receipt-wise computation tables). It must be filed online (digital signature or EVC) and cannot be edited after e-verification.
Additional Details for Form 39 Part B — Gratuity / Retrenchment / Pension Commutation Optional
⚠ This calculator computes Section 89(1)/157(1) relief for salary arrears/advance only (Annexure I equivalent), using the same Table A engine as Form 10E. If you also received gratuity, retrenchment compensation, or commuted pension this Tax Year, enter the gross amount below purely for disclosure in Part B — their relief is NOT computed by this calculator (each has its own separate computation method under Rule 73) and should be worked out on the official e-filing portal.
